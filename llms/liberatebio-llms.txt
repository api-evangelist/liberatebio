# Liberate Bio

> Liberate Bio is a genetic medicine company developing in vivo cell therapies that reach cell populations beyond the liver. Its RAPTOR discovery platform screens lipid nanoparticle (LNP) delivery vehicles inside living organisms to find carriers that deliver genetic payloads to immune cells in blood, bone marrow, and the spleen. The company applies this delivery capability to in vivo CAR-M (chimeric antigen receptor macrophage) programs in hematologic oncology and autoimmune disease.
